Résumé

I write low-level, performance-minded code, and I like implementing fundamental things from scratch. Most of the detail lives in the projects.

Skills

languages
Rust (primary), C, Python, JavaScript, Haskell.
systems & performance
Multithreading with lock-free atomics, cache-aware memory layout, flamegraph profiling, spatial data structures.
graphics & simulation
Real-time rendering on the pixels/wgpu stack, WebAssembly ports, fluid and particle simulation.
math & numerics
FFT, reverse-mode autodiff, numerical methods for PDEs, computational geometry, constraint satisfaction.
tools & hardware
Hakyll, Git, KiCad PCB design, QMK firmware.

Selected projects

eulr Rust, WebAssembly
Eulerian fluid simulation built from the equations up, with pressure projection and semi-Lagrangian advection. Ported to WebAssembly to run in the browser. Live demo.
pcls Rust
Multithreaded Verlet particle simulation: 50,000+ particles at 60 FPS with uniform-grid collision detection and lock-free atomic updates.
wavz Rust
WAV player and audio visualizer driven by a hand-written Cooley-Tukey FFT, no DSP libraries.
karp Rust
Reverse-mode automatic differentiation engine: backpropagation over a dynamic computation graph, training a small multi-layer perceptron.
babygrand Hardware
A 31-key mechanical keyboard designed end to end: a caseless PCB in KiCad, custom QMK firmware, and a community group buy.
